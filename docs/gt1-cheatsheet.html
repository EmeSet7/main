GT League
Four championships, raced in order. Every course is driven in its normal direction. No car restrictions in this league — bring whatever you're comfortable with.

BEST CAR

A 1990 Mazda RX-7 FC GT-X or an MA70 Toyota Supra Turbo Limited from the used lot — both sit comfortably inside a starting ~10,000 Cr budget and are far quicker than anything the AI fields here. Basically anything with a pulse wins the Sunday Cup; save your money rather than overspending on this one.

Champion's prize car: a Sprinter Trueno GT-Apex (AE86) '83 or Mazdaspeed Demio A-spec, depending on region. Neither is worth keeping — sell it straight to the dealer.

  • Pole position bonus10,000

BEST CAR

Your best fully Race-Modified special/road car — a Race-Modified Toyota Supra RM, Nissan Skyline GT-R RM, or a genuine Special Model like the TWR Jaguar XJ220. Six full-length races back to back means reliability and stable handling across both fast (High Speed Ring) and technical (Trial Mountain, Special Stage 11) sections matter more than peak top speed.

No prize car — instead you unlock GT Hi-Fi mode (60fps on Clubman Stage 5, SS Route 5 & SS Route 11) and the game's ending credits roll. This is the actual "finish the game" moment.
Special Events
13 events split across skill challenges, manufacturer derbies, speed contests, and three endurance races. This is where most of the campaign's money and hours actually go. Required license is shown per event.

Licenses
Three tiers, each with 8 pass/fail driving tests. Clear all 8 to unlock the license; get gold on all 8 in the same license to also win a prize car. Each attempt burns one in-game day.
LicenseUnlocksAll-gold prize car
B-LicenseSunday Cup, FF/FR/4WD/Lightweight Special EventsDodge Concept Car (can't be sold — no listed price)
A-LicenseClubman Cup, GT Cup, manufacturer derbies, Megaspeed, Normal Car ContestTRD 3000GT

B-License gold times run from roughly 23.6s to 1:19.5 depending on the test — test B-8 (the Trial Mountain tunnel section on a Skyline GT-R) is the one most players fail first. Budget several attempts for it; there's no penalty beyond time spent.
